AutoPost Bluetooth Setup

Connect over Bluetooth to give this device the right WiFi. Works even when its own setup network will not appear.

This browser cannot do Bluetooth

On an iPhone or iPad, use the free nRF Connect or LightBlue app instead. Connect to the device named AutoPost-Setup-…, open the service starting a5f10000, and write this text to the characteristic ending 0003:

Writesetupcode|YourWiFiName|YourPassword

Then read the characteristic ending 0001 to see how it went.

The device must be powered on and within about ten metres. It appears as AutoPost‑Setup‑ followed by four characters.
